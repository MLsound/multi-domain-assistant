In the event of a severe weather or flood warning, the HEMS activates an emergency safety mode. Users must disconnect all lithium-ion battery-powered devices and, if possible, move them to higher ground to prevent water-induced short circuits. If a storm causes an outage, the system performs strict load shedding, disabling all Tier 2 and Tier 3 devices (e.g., HVAC, EV chargers) to preserve the remaining battery capacity for critical Tier 1 loads like medical devices and internet routers.
