The HEMS utilizes geofencing to trigger a 'Home' or 'Away' profile based on resident proximity. When the geofence indicates all users have left, the system switches to a minimal standby profile: HVAC setpoints are adjusted to 58°F in winter and 82°F in summer, lighting is disabled, and non-essential circuits (e.g., pool pumps) are powered down. Pre-arrival automation activates HVAC 45 minutes before return to ensure comfort upon entry.
