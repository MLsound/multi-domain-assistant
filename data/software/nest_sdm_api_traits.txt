The Google Nest SDM API utilize traits for HEMS orchestration:
- sdm.devices.traits.Temperature: Reports ambientTemperatureCelsius.
- sdm.devices.traits.Humidity: Reports ambientHumidityPercent.
- sdm.devices.traits.ThermostatMode: Manages states (HEAT, COOL, OFF).
Commands are executed via POST requests to the executeCommand endpoint.
