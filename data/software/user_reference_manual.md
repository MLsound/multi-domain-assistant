# Home Energy Assistant: User Reference Manual

## Introduction
The Home Energy Assistant helps optimize your household energy footprint by connecting real-time local weather tracking with your solar power system and home battery. This manual explains how the system works, how it processes environmental variables, and how it manages computing tasks to keep your home running efficiently.

---

## How the System Works
The assistant continuously monitors your home's energy consumption, solar panel performance, and local weather forecasts. By analyzing these data points, the system makes smart decisions about when to use solar power, when to store energy in your battery, and when to pull electricity from the utility grid.



---

## Smart Computing and Resource Management
To ensure smooth operation, the energy assistant manages computing power using a system of "Compute Units." Heavy calculations are balanced so the system never slows down or crashes.

Different tasks require varying levels of computational effort:
* **Weather and Data Updates:** The system collects outdoor temperature and rain forecasts. This is a low-effort task that keeps the operational data fresh.
* **Solar Energy Estimation:** The assistant calculates how much power your solar panels will generate. This medium-effort task factors in sunlight and heat.
* **Energy Optimization:** The system calculates the best time to charge or discharge your battery. This high-effort task evaluates your current electrical needs against expected solar output.

---

## Solar Power and Temperature Effects
Solar panels generate electricity from sunlight, but their efficiency is heavily influenced by the outdoor temperature. 

* **The Heat Factor:** Solar panels operate best in bright, cool conditions. As the temperature of the solar cells rises, their electrical efficiency decreases. 
* **Cell Temperature Tracking:** The assistant tracks the direct heat of your solar panels rather than just the air temperature. If the air is hot or the sun is intense, the system automatically adjusts the expected solar energy output downward to reflect realistic generation.

---

## Intelligent Battery Management
The core goal of the system is to optimize your home battery's energy storage. Based on the data collected from your solar panels and local weather patterns, the system automatically switches between three major operational modes:

### Charging Mode
When your solar panels generate more electricity than your home is currently consuming, the assistant routes the excess energy to charge your battery. If rain or heavy cloud cover is forecasted, the system prioritizes charging the battery while sunlight is still available.

### Discharging Mode
When your home's energy usage exceeds the power being generated by your solar panels (such as in the evening or during a storm), the assistant draws power from the battery instead of the utility grid. This saves money by avoiding expensive grid power.

### Idle Mode
If your battery is fully charged or has reached its minimum safe energy level, the system goes into a resting state. Power then flows normally from the solar panels directly to your appliances, or from the grid if solar power is unavailable.
