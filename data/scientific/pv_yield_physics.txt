Photovoltaic efficiency is highly sensitive to environmental variables. Crystalline silicon efficiency degrades by 0.4% to 0.5% for every degree Celsius above ^\circ C$. The module temperature is estimated using the formula: {mod} = T_{amb} + \frac{G}{800}(TNOCT - 20)$. High humidity (85% relative humidity at ^\circ C$) can initiate internal circuitry corrosion within 1,000 hours.
